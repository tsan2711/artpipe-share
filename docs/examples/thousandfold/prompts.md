# Thousandfold — prompt key art so sánh A / B / C

Cùng cảnh, cùng khung để so công bằng: ngã tư thành phố nhỏ, góc nhìn từ trên xuống chéo ~50°, bầy ~2000 con tràn vào từ góc dưới trái,
một con khổng lồ (robot cảnh sát cao 3 tầng) ở giữa. Tỉ lệ 16:9, 2 seed mỗi hướng. Không chữ, không logo, không UI.

Phần chung (dán sau mỗi prompt):
`top-down isometric 3/4 view, small stylised city intersection with cars, shops, trees, a giant three-storey police robot in the centre, a swarm of about two thousand tiny creatures flooding in from the bottom-left corner and climbing the robot, game key art, clean readable shapes, strong silhouette of the swarm as one mass, soft global illumination, high detail, no text, no UI, no logo, 16:9`

## A. Slime nuốt thành phố
`A swarm of glossy candy-coloured slime blobs (magenta, lime, cyan, each blob a tiny round droplet with two dot eyes), merging into a single flowing liquid mass that swallows cars and lamp posts, wet specular highlights, jelly translucency, playful but slightly gross, saturated slime against a muted beige-grey city` + phần chung

## B. Mực màu tô lại thế giới
`The city is completely desaturated, monochrome grey like an old photograph; a swarm of tiny ink-creatures in vivid neon paint colours (orange, violet, teal) pours in, and everything the swarm has touched is splashed back into full vivid colour, a sharp boundary between grey and colour, paint drips and splatters, painterly ink texture` + phần chung

## C. Bầy thú nhỏ lông xù
`A swarm of tiny round fluffy fur-ball critters with big shiny eyes (cream, peach, soft brown, like Ghibli soot sprites but warm-coloured), rolling and tumbling as one fluffy wave, soft fur rim light, cosy and cute, warm sunset light over a muted blue-grey city` + phần chung
